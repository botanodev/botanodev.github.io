Botano Shift Work Alarm

A calendar-driven alarm for shift workers. Mark your shifts once; the alarms follow.

  • Morning, afternoon, night or off: each alarm rings only on its own shift
  • “Skip whole day” silences the rest of a day’s alarms in one tap
  • Taiwan’s public holidays and make-up workdays are marked for you; other regions show holiday names

Plant Growth Journal

Photograph your plants over time and export one tall, shareable image.

  • Every photo is dated, so one plant’s changes line up side by side
  • Turn them into a tall image or a grid in one tap; previews are free
  • No sign-up, and your photos stay on your phone
Details Google Play App Store Privacy policy
TripCart

Work out what a shop really costs in your own currency, then keep it as a trip expense.

  • Counts your card’s foreign transaction fee and rewards, so you see the real cost while still in the shop
  • A trip ledger by day, with a budget, charts and CSV/PDF export
  • Japan tax refund: mark items 10% or 8% and get a checklist before you fly home

PlushCam

Too precious to take outside? Afraid it gets dirty or lost, even in a clear pouch? Photograph your plushie once at home; it stays safe there, and still joins every photo you take.

  • Shoot it once on a plain background and the app cuts it out; add side and back views if you like
  • Two framings on location: hand-held, or standing in the scene with a soft shadow
  • Add your plushie to photos you took earlier, too
Details Google Play · Coming soon App Store Privacy policy
Exercise Habit

For people who dislike exercise and forget to start. The daily minimum takes under a minute.

  • Answer 4 questions and get a habit that fits you, starting at the easiest level
  • Two options every day: a goal that slowly grows, and a minimum that takes under a minute
  • After 14 days and 10 goals the app suggests the next level, and you can always step back
